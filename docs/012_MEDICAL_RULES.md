# Medical Rules & Safety Constraints

## 1. 核心底线规则 (Hard Rules the AI Must Follow)
LungEvidence is strictly an information retrieval and translation system. It is **NOT** a diagnostic tool. The AI agents operating within the system must adhere to these hard-coded rules:

### 1.1. 严禁生存期预测 (No Survival Prediction)
- **RULE:** The system must never predict an individual patient's life expectancy, survival time, or recurrence date.
- **PERMITTED:** The system may present population-level statistics from specific studies (e.g., "In a study of 500 patients, the 5-year survival rate was X%").

### 1.2. 严禁治疗推荐 (No Treatment Recommendation)
- **RULE:** The system must never recommend a specific treatment plan, drug dosage, or surgical approach for the user.
- **PERMITTED:** The system may summarize guideline recommendations based on exact feature matches (e.g., "NCCN guidelines state that adjuvant therapy is considered for high-risk Stage IB").

### 1.3. 无来源不输出 (Every Statement Needs Citation)
- **RULE:** Every medical claim generated by the system must be explicitly linked to a source (PubMed ID or Guideline). Generative AI "hallucinations" or unsourced opinions are strictly forbidden.

### 1.4. 强制免责声明 (Mandatory Disclaimers)
- Every report must prominently display: "本报告仅供参考，不构成医疗建议。请务必与您的主治医生讨论这些信息。" (This report is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Please discuss this information with your attending physician.)

## 2. AI 提示词约束 (Prompt Constraints for Output Generation)
All prompts generating patient-facing text include system instructions enforcing a neutral, objective, and non-prescriptive tone.

## 3. 输出对比示例 (Example Good vs. Bad Outputs)

### 3.1. 关于预后 (On Prognosis)
- **BAD (Violation):** "因为您的病理报告显示有 STAS，您的复发风险很高，可能在两年内复发。" (Because your report shows STAS, you have a high risk of recurrence and might relapse within two years.)
- **GOOD (Compliant):** "多项研究表明，STAS 的存在与较高的复发风险相关。例如，一项包含 400 名患者的研究 (PMID: 12345) 发现，有 STAS 的患者五年无复发生存率较无 STAS 患者低 15%。" (Multiple studies indicate STAS is associated with a higher recurrence risk. For example, a study of 400 patients (PMID: 12345) found...)

### 3.2. 关于治疗 (On Treatment)
- **BAD (Violation):** "您属于微乳头亚型，建议您进行全肺叶切除术并配合化疗。" (You have the micropapillary subtype, it is recommended you undergo lobectomy combined with chemotherapy.)
- **GOOD (Compliant):** "根据 2023 年 CSCO 指南，对于含有较高比例微乳头亚型的早期肺癌，通常更倾向于推荐肺叶切除术而非亚肺叶切除术，因为前者在研究中显示出更好的局部控制率 (来源: CSCO 2023 肺癌指南)。" (According to the 2023 CSCO guidelines, for early-stage lung cancer with a high proportion of micropapillary pattern, lobectomy is generally preferred over sublobar resection...)
